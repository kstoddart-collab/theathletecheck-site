Identity Intelligence

Coaches and leaders today aren’t just managing performance — you’re managing pressure, transition, mindset, and identity stability. The Athlete Check™ gives you the clarity you’ve been missing. It measures the internal variables that determine whether an athlete will thrive, struggle, or transfer.

What The Athlete Check™ Measures

  • Leadership Identity Influence, stability, and team impact
  • Emotional Resilience Handling adversity without spiraling
  • Boundaries & Values Decision-making under pressure
  • Physical Presence Confidence, discipline, and self-ownership
  • Relationships & Belonging Locker room fit and cultural impact
  • Purpose & Conviction Internal drive vs. external pressure
  • Financial Confidence NIL readiness and life skills
  • Legacy & Vision Identity beyond the sport

Why Coaches, ADs & Agents Use It

  • Recruiting intelligence and transition readiness
  • Culture protection and leadership identification
  • Transfer portal risk reduction
  • Development planning and athlete support
  • Neutral, data-driven communication framework
