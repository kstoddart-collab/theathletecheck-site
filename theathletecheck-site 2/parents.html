01 / Why I built this

I looked unstoppable.
I was falling apart.

I graduated from The Citadel in 1997 and went into cybersecurity. For 27 years, I chased promotions, quotas, and the external markers that tell you you’re succeeding.

The higher I climbed, the less I recognized myself. I looked composed and capable while privately dealing with depression, burnout, and a body that was breaking down. I thought asking for help was something a leader could never do.

Rebuilding meant learning who I was beyond what I achieved. It took a plan, daily reps, and people who wouldn’t let me quit. I’ve finished ten Ironman races and survived Stage 3 prostate cancer. I earned my MBA from The Citadel and am completing my Master’s in Clinical Mental Health Counseling at Liberty University.

I want young people to build that foundation earlier — before a transition, a setback, or the end of a season forces the question.

“I’m trying to reach your son or daughter 25 years earlier than anyone reached me.”

Confidence under pressure

Build the discipline, resilience, and habits to respond to setbacks and keep moving.

Readiness for what’s next

Practice accountability, strengthen relationships, and approach the next chapter with direction.
