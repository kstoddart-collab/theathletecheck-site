OUR MISSION

The Athlete Check is a readiness and identity platform created by student athletes, for student athletes. Founded by Luke Stenson and Wyatt Porch, both Division I athletes, the platform was born out of the IronMind Summer Internship Program — a high performance environment built around identity, leadership, and personal development.

The mission is simple: Help student athletes understand where they truly stand, and prepare them for what comes next.
